Privacy Policy

Last Updated & Effective Date: November 23, 2026

Welcome to WINCO Connect ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting the privacy and confidentiality of our members and users. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding the collection, use, storage, disclosure, and protection of information obtained through the WINCO Connect mobile applications (available on Android and iOS) and our official website (https://wincoconnect.com).

1. Information We Collect

To deliver an exclusive B2B networking experience for infrastructure professionals, contractors, and consultants, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account & Profile Information: Full name, professional email address, phone number, company name, industry category/specialization, office address, GST/business registration details, and profile avatar.
  • Networking & Referral Records: Business referral slips (Given and Received), project specifications, estimated contract values, TYFCB (Thank You For Closed Business) details, and conclave RSVP attendance.
  • Technical & Device Data: Mobile device model, operating system version, app version, IP address, and standard diagnostic/crash analytics to maintain system reliability.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is strictly used for legitimate business networking operations:

  • To authenticate and manage your verified WINCO membership account.
  • To display your professional credentials in the exclusive Member Directory.
  • To enable real-time passing, tracking, and notification of high-ticket business referrals.
  • To facilitate joint venture consortium building on mega infrastructure tenders.
  • To coordinate meeting schedules, venues, and RSVPs for fortnightly regional chapter conclaves.
  • To maintain app security, prevent fraud, and verify member identity.

3. Data Protection & Security

We implement enterprise-grade security protocols to protect your personal and commercial data:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the mobile application and our servers are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2/1.3 and HTTPS).
  • Secure Native Storage: Authentication tokens and sensitive credentials on devices are protected via Android Keystore and iOS Keychain.
  • Access Control: Administrative access to member databases is restricted strictly to authorized secretariat personnel.

4. Information Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or business information to third parties or marketing brokers.

Your business contact details (name, company, phone, email) are visible only to fellow authenticated and verified WINCO members within the private network to facilitate direct business collaboration.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain member information for the duration of active membership in WINCO. If you wish to delete your account or request the permanent deletion of your profile data:

  • You may request account deletion directly through the mobile application settings, or
  • Email our administrative secretariat at info@wincoconnect.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". Upon verification, your profile and associated credentials will be permanently purged within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

WINCO Connect is strictly a professional B2B platform intended for working business executives and contractors aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from individuals under 18.

7. Policy Updates

We may periodically update this policy to reflect enhancements in our services or changes in legal regulations. Material modifications will be notified through the mobile application or our official website.
